========================================================================

QUESTION
  The alpha x lambda sweep (tests/sweep_alpha_lambda) found alpha 0.1, lambda 0.3 much better than 0.03.
  Does switching the running Horde to alpha 0.1 improve seek-light and avoid-obstacles?

ANSWER
  No. Both diverged (max|theta| seek 1.3e7, avoid 2.0e5 by 9.0M). Cause not established here;
  the next stage (tests/horde_diverged_trace_rho) traced it to uncapped rho in the trace.

CONCLUSIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - avoid was already collapsing before the alpha change (old alpha): 0.30x at 6.675M, 0.79x at 6.975M,
    -0.44x at 7.8M. The change made both demons diverge.
  - The sweep with the same settings from ZERO weights stayed stable for 1M (max|theta| ~2.5), so
    divergence needs a longer run and/or the warm start from the old weights.
  - Cause not established in this stage; there are no intermediate weight logs.

WHAT IT LED TO
  - seek/avoid reset to 0 at 9.0M and a max|theta| line added to the log (tests/horde_diverged_trace_rho).

CAVEATS

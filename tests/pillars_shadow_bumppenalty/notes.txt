 Horde run 0 - 6M: pillars + hard light shadow + bump fix + bump penalty in the avoid reward
========================================================================

QUESTION
  First Horde run on the current arena (2 pillars that cast hard light shadows), with the fixed bump sensor
  and a bump penalty in the avoid reward. Do the control demons learn, and can seek + avoid be combined?

ANSWER
  spin and avoid learned (3.85x, 1.51x random at 6M); seek-light did not (0.92x at 6M, worse than its
  early peak). Diagnosis: the sum-of-4-lights reward barely changes with heading, so seek never learned to
  turn. Led to the FL+FR reward and new brightness formula at 6M.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONCLUSIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - seek-light mostly drives forward/backward instead of turning toward the light
    (it turns the wrong way as often as the right way).
  - Likely cause: the sum-of-4 reward changes only 1.5x with heading (0.30 - 0.45 at (2.5, 2.5));
    a front-only reward FL+FR changes 0 -> 0.45.
  - Adding avoid cuts bumps tenfold (143.9 -> 12.7 per 1k at w 0.75) but nobody reaches the light,
    because seek itself is weak.

WHAT IT LED TO
  - At 6M: brightness 0.25/(0.25+d^2) and seek reward FL+FR (continued in tests/horde_12M_capped_trace).

CAVEATS
  - 10-start evals are very noisy.
  - Not comparable to earlier pillar runs (no shadow, old bump flag, no bump penalty).

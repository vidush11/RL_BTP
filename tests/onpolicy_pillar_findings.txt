On-policy Sarsa(lambda) light seeker in the pillar world (4 IR + 4 light sensors, 2-axis compass).
All evaluated from 80 uniform random start positions/headings, 300 steps each.

D sum reward + random restart / 500 steps 15%           1.25 m    12 / 24 / 6               almost never turns (2%)

F sum reward + restarts + 0.5 s decisions 57%           0.82 m    46 / 18 / 9               BEST: action held 5 steps (paper's 500 ms)
G front reward + restarts + 0.5 s         18%           0.98 m    14 / 21 / 17

Conclusions:
1. One continuous life -> robot settles in one spot and never learns the rest of the arena; restarts fix coverage.
2. 0.1 s decisions -> action effects too similar and 2 s look-ahead too short; 0.5 s decisions fix it.
3. Sum-of-4-lights reward beats front-only and max-of-4.
4. Remaining failures mostly at pillar 1: seek-light alone ignores obstacles (motivates seek + avoid combination).
Correction: the earlier open-arena on-policy result (0.29 m) was evaluated from states next to the light, so it was inflated.
